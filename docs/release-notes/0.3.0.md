## Main features

- Video and audio playback with pause, seeking, playback speed, and fullscreen controls.
- Playlists and chapter navigation.
- Audio and subtitle track selection, with subtitle timing adjustments.
- Frame-by-frame playback, aspect ratio, crop, and rotation controls.
- Screenshots, saved preferences, and compact Music Mode.
- Volume from 0 to 100% by default, with an optional boost up to 200%.
