Riina for Windows 11 (64-bit)

Setup installs Riina and its playback engine for your Windows account. Administrator
rights and a separate mpv installation are not required.

A Start menu shortcut is included. A desktop shortcut is optional. Setup does not
change your default media player or file associations.

Uninstall Riina through Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps. Your preferences,
playback history and media files are preserved.
